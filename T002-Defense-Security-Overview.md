# 🛡️ Introduction to Defensive Security (Blue Teaming)

## 📌 Overview
Defensive Security, also known as **Blue Teaming**, focuses on protecting an organisation’s networks, systems, and data from cyber threats. Defensive security teams play a critical role in monitoring, detecting, responding to, and preventing cyber attacks across organisations worldwide.

When defensive security fails, the consequences can be severe—ranging from data breaches and service disruptions to massive financial penalties and loss of public trust.

---

## 🚨 Real-World Security Breaches

### ✈️ British Airways (2018)
- **Data leaked:** Personal details and payment card information  
- **Affected users:** 400,000+ customers  
- **Penalty:** £20 million fine  

### 🏨 Marriott International (2020)
- **Data leaked:** Passport information, payment card details, names  
- **Affected users:** 334 million customers  
- **Cause:** Outdated systems  
- **Penalty:** $52 million settlement  

### 🖥️ Advanced Computer Software Group (2022)
- **Data leaked:** Medical records and phone numbers  
- **Affected users:** 79,904 individuals  
- **Cause:** Weak security policies  
- **Penalty:** £3.07 million fine  

### 🧬 23andMe (2023)
- **Data leaked:** Names, addresses, health records, DNA data  
- **Affected users:** 7+ million users  
- **Cause:** Poor security policies  
- **Penalty:** £2.31 million fine  

### 📡 SK Telecom (2024)
- **Data leaked:** Personal data  
- **Affected users:** ~27 million users  
- **Cause:** Poorly configured servers  
- **Penalty:** $97 million fine

## 🔍 Key Areas of Defensive Security

### 👀 Monitoring and Detection
- Continuous observation of networks and systems  
- Detects suspicious activities (e.g., login from another country)

### 🚑 Incident Response
- Rapid response when a threat is confirmed  
- Focuses on containment, removal, and recovery  

### 🧠 Threat Intelligence
- Collecting information about attackers, tools, and methods  
- Helps anticipate and prevent future attacks  

### 🧩 Vulnerability Management
- Identifying and fixing software or system weaknesses  
- Uses manual reviews and automated scanning tools  

### 🔬 Investigation and Analysis
- Analysing events to distinguish normal vs. malicious activity  
- Deep investigation to uncover attacker behaviour and insights

## 👥 Roles in a Defensive Security Team

### 🖥️ SOC Analyst – *Bob*
- Monitors systems and network activity  
- Identifies suspicious or unexpected behaviour  
- Acts as the frontline defender  

### 🚨 Incident Responder – *Aaliyah*
- Investigates and responds to active incidents  
- Stops attackers in real time  
- Shares lessons learned to prevent recurrence  

### 🛠️ Security Engineer – *Zoe*
- Builds and maintains security tools and systems  
- Supports monitoring, investigation, and analysis  

### 🧾 Digital Forensics Specialist – *Bill*
- Gathers and analyses digital evidence  
- Determines what happened and how attackers operated

## 🧱 Defence in Depth

Organisations use multiple layers of security rather than relying on a single control. This approach is known as **Defence in Depth**—similar to layers of a castle or an onion.

### 🧑‍🏫 Employee Training
- Employees are frequent targets of attacks  
- Training helps detect phishing and social engineering  

### 🚨 Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)
- Monitor networks and systems  
- Alert teams to suspicious activity  

### 🔥 Firewalls
- Control what traffic enters or leaves the network  
- Act as the first line of defence  

### 📜 Security Policies
- Define proper system usage  
- Enforce strong passwords and block risky websites  
- Reduce damage even if human error occurs

## 🏢 Security Operations Centre (SOC)

### 🔐 What is a SOC?
The **Security Operations Centre (SOC)** is the central hub of defensive security. It operates 24/7 and is staffed by security professionals who protect the organisation’s systems, networks, and data.

### 🕒 Typical SOC Activities
- Reviewing security alerts  
- Investigating anomalies  
- Responding to incidents  

SOC teams act as the **eyes and ears** of an organisation’s cybersecurity posture.

---

## 📡 SIEM: The Defensive Security Radar

**SIEM (Security Information and Event Management)** systems collect and centralise logs and security data from:
- Servers
- Workstations
- Network devices
- Security tools  

### ✅ Why SIEMs Matter
- Provide real-time visibility across IT systems  
- Enable faster investigation and collaboration  
- Help teams quickly understand what’s happening  

SIEMs are a **critical component** of modern defensive security operations.
