# 🛡️ Want a Career in Cybersecurity?
## Explore What That Could Look Like for You 🔐

Cybersecurity offers **many career paths**, ranging from:
- ⚔️ **Offensive Security** – breaking into systems (ethically)
- 🛡️ **Defensive Security** – protecting organisations from attacks

You **don’t need a specific background** to start. If you enjoy:
- 🧩 Solving problems  
- 🔍 Being curious  
- ⚙️ Understanding how systems work  

There’s likely a cybersecurity role that fits you.

---

## 🚀 Why Choose a Career in Cybersecurity?

- 📈 **High Demand** – Over **3.5 million unfilled roles** worldwide  
- 💰 **Strong Salaries** – Competitive pay, even at entry level  
- 📚 **Constant Learning** – Technology and threats evolve rapidly  

Ready to explore some of the **leading cybersecurity roles**? Let’s begin 👇

## 🔵 Security Analyst  
### Detail-Focused, Curious, and Love Puzzles 🧠

Security Analysts are often called the **digital defenders** of an organisation and are part of the **blue team**.

### 🔍 What They Do
- Monitor devices and networks for suspicious activity  
- Investigate security alerts and incidents  
- Determine whether activity is legitimate or malicious  
- Respond appropriately to protect the organisation  

📌 **Example Incident**:  
An employee usually logs in from London but suddenly accesses systems from another country — this triggers an investigation.

### 🗓️ A Typical Day Includes
- Monitoring network and device activity  
- Investigating unusual logins or alerts  
- Piecing together what happened, when, and how  
- Collaborating with other teams to improve defences  

### 📈 Career Progression
Security Analyst is a **broad entry point** into defensive security. You can later specialise in:
- 🔎 Threat Hunting  
- 🚨 Incident Response  
- 🧪 Malware Analysis  

➡️ Incident Responders handle active attacks, while Malware Analysts study malicious tools and code.

✅ **This role could be for you!**

## 🏗️ Security Engineer  
### If You Enjoy Problem-Solving and Building Things 🛠️

Security Engineers are the **architects of cybersecurity**. They design, build, and maintain systems that protect networks and devices.

### 🧩 What They Do
- Develop and maintain security systems and tools  
- Manage technologies like **Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)**  
- Stay updated on the latest hacker techniques  
- Ensure systems are secure against vulnerabilities  

📌 **Example**:  
An IDS works like a **digital security camera**, detecting and alerting teams about suspicious activity.

### 🗓️ A Typical Day Includes
- Designing and maintaining security infrastructure  
- Documenting security processes and procedures  
- Assessing risks and system vulnerabilities  
- Ensuring applications and systems are well-protected  

### 📈 Career Progression
Already a specialised role, but you can go deeper into:
- ☁️ Cloud Security  
- 📱 Application Security  

🎯 Skills from this role also transfer well into **offensive security**.

✅ **This may align with you!**

## 🔴 Penetration Tester (Ethical Hacker)  
### Methodical and Curious Thinkers 🕵️‍♂️

Also known as **pentesters** or **ethical hackers**, penetration testers safely try to break into systems to identify weaknesses.

### 🧪 What They Do
- Simulate real-world attacks (with permission)  
- Identify vulnerabilities in systems, networks, and applications  
- Test whether vulnerabilities can be exploited  
- Help organisations fix issues before attackers exploit them  

📜 All testing is done under a formal agreement called an **engagement**.

### 🗓️ A Typical Day Includes
- Testing networks, systems, and websites  
- Performing security audits and assessments  
- Analysing results and writing detailed reports  
- Advising organisations on how to prevent future attacks  

### 📈 Career Progression
Penetration testing is a strong foundation for **offensive security** careers. You can specialise in:
- 🌐 Network Testing  
- 🖥️ Web Application Testing  

🚨 Advanced Path:
- **Red Teaming** – simulating full-scale, long-term attacks across an organisation, sometimes including physical intrusion  

➡️ Red teaming is a **senior-level progression** from pentesting.
